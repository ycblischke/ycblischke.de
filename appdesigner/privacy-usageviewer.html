✦ Claude Usage Viewer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Controller

Yves Claudio Blischke
Email: ycblischke@gmail.com

2. Data Processed

Anthropic API Key
Stored exclusively on your device (SharedPreferences). Never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

Requests to the Anthropic API
The app sends your API key directly to api.anthropic.com to retrieve rate limit information (remaining tokens, requests, reset time). No personal data is collected by us.

Local History Data
Token and request counts are stored locally for up to 7 days (5h and weekly overview). This data does not leave your device.

3. Third Parties

The app communicates exclusively with api.anthropic.com. Anthropic's privacy policy applies: anthropic.com/privacy

4. No Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

5. Data Deletion

All stored data (API key, history) is permanently deleted upon uninstalling the app.
